# GhostPay
GHOST PAY - Verified now. Gone later.

<img width="529" height="137" alt="grafik" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a7c3df49-9766-4421-80ec-f24842db1f6b" />

Requirements:
- Windows 10 or 11 required (the .py file in the installation folder also runs on Linux)

Main Features:
- One of the most ingenious tools ever written!
- Bitcoin stealers are a thing of the past!
- Create real, verified crypto transactions, confirmed on the
  blockchain!
- Pay with crypto and receive your product as usual once the
  transaction is confirmed
- Then, when the timer runs out, the magic happens—Gone! All traces of your
  transaction are gone—no entry on the blockchain, no traces on our servers. Only you and the other party know that this transaction ever took place! (Or maybe not...)
